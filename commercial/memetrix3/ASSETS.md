# THE MEMETRIX III: sourced assets

Every new location is dressed from open-licensed libraries. They are downloaded by `fetch_assets.py` into `$W/ext3` and
are not committed to this repo.

| Source | Where | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Polygonal Mind *tomb-chaser-2* (neonwave pagoda) | github.com/ToxSam/cc0-models-Polygonal-Mind | CC0 | The Neon Shrine |
| Polygonal Mind *transit* (tower station, track, elevator) | same | CC0 | The Maglev station and track. The train body is a new procedural build, because the pack's train is a small pod |
| Polygonal Mind *aero-system* (floating islands, airship, rings) | same | CC0 | The Sky Docks |
| Polygonal Mind *crystal-crossroads* (crystals, arches, ruins) | same | CC0 | The Crystal Desert |
| Polygonal Mind *xyz* (60 rigged geometric creatures) | same | CC0 | The Liquidators (bots), with a red sell-eye material added |
| Quaternius Modular Sci-Fi pack (.blend) | github.com/Malcolmnixon/Quaternius-Modular-Scifi-Pack | CC0 | The Exchange |
| Poly Haven models (glTF, 1k) | api.polyhaven.com (repo: github.com/Poly-Haven) | CC0 | The Server Farm, Capitulation Street |
| Poly Haven HDRIs (2k) | same | CC0 | Skies and reflections for the Sky Docks, Crystal Desert and street |
| Oculus Audio Pack 01 (Meta Open Sound) | github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound | **CC BY 4.0** | Fights, glass, metal, the train, lasers, drones, the Whale's laugh |

## Required credit (in the closing titles)
> "Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

CC0 assets need no credit, but the closing titles thank Polygonal Mind, Quaternius and Poly Haven anyway.

## Notes
- Sponsor and brand logos baked into some Polygonal Mind textures (the transit sponsor rings, for example) are not used,
  or are re-textured.
- The selection is listed in `fetch_assets.py`. To try another asset, add its name there and run the fetch for that set
  again.
