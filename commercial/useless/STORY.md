# NO UTILITY: a 35-second Meme Royale short

## What Supercell did, and what this borrows
- **Clash Royale, "30 Seconds of Innocence" (DAVID NY):** a Golem guards a small ward. It lets a butterfly
  lead the ward away to safety, then turns alone to face a skeleton horde. There is no dialogue and no text.
  Warm light surrounds the one being protected, and cold light marks the danger. The audience knows the cost
  and the ward never does. That gap is the whole emotional hit.
- **Clash Royale, "30 Seconds Left":** the drama comes from the last seconds of a real match, the clutch moment
  every player has lived through.
- **Clash of Clans, Hog Rider spots and *Lost & Crowned*:** the cheapest, silliest units get personalities.
  Players come to love the grunt, not the legendary.
- **The lessons:** no words; one small character who wants something; a clock; a sacrifice nobody sees; a
  closing image the viewer completes.

## Why USELESS
- The game's own card blurb is the premise: *"Four gold coins with no utility."* It's a common card that costs
  2, the cheapest in the deck.
- The game's Pro Tips video says *"One LMAO! wipes it out."* The game literally calls it fodder.
- It's a gold coin with big eyes, white gloves and sneakers. It reads as a kid even without a face rig, so it's
  the most sympathetic shape in the roster.
- Its model has every clip the story needs: idle, ready, walk, run, attack1–3, hit, die, win, lose, emote. The
  model is used as-is, with its own clips only.
- Supporting cast, all with the same clip set: THE GOAT (the enemy legendary), GIGA and The Whale (the stars who
  get picked first).

## The story (no dialogue; music, sound effects and on-screen game UI only)
| Time | Shot | Clip(s) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Before a match, a stone staging hall in warm torchlight. Cards stand in a row. A hand-of-god light picks GIGA, then The Whale; each walks out into the roar of the arena. | GIGA/Whale `walk`; USELESS `ready` each time, then `idle` |
| 5–9 s | Montage of match after match (the arena light cycling from day to night). Every time, USELESS bounces `ready` and is left behind. The last time it doesn't even get up. | `ready` → `lose` (the slump) |
| 9–14 s | Final match, through the gate: our side is losing. GIGA and The Whale go down. The clock reads **0:12**. Our king tower is cracked, with a sliver of HP. THE GOAT lowers its horns. | GIGA `die`, Whale `die`, GOAT `ready` |
| 14–16 s | The elixir (COPIUM) bar holds exactly 2. Only one card is affordable. The light falls on USELESS for the first time. It looks up. | USELESS `emote` (it can't believe it) |
| 16–20 s | It runs across the bridge alone, small in a huge cold-blue arena. The GOAT starts its charge from the other side. | USELESS `run`; GOAT `run` |
| 20–27 s | It plants itself in front of the tower and hits the GOAT: tiny sparks, **-1**, **-1**. The GOAT swats it down; it gets back up; again. The clock: **0:03… 0:02… 0:01**. | `attack1/2/3`, `hit`, `ready`, repeat |
| 27–29 s | **0:00**. The horn. The tower stands at **1 HP**. USELESS sways once, then falls. A single gold coin rolls in a half circle and lies still. | USELESS `die`; GOAT `lose` |
| 29–32 s | The victory screen: **VICTORY**, with GIGA and The Whale as MVP. Nobody mentions it. Then a quiet cut to the next deck screen: a thumb drags the USELESS card into **slot 1**. | UI over a soft wide of the empty arena |
| 32–35 s | The end card over a still of USELESS in warm light (its `win` pose, frozen): **NO CARD IS USELESS.** Meme Royale, Play free, memeroyale.imaginestudio.fun | `win`, held |

## Notes
- **Lighting rule (from Innocence):** USELESS gets warm light only when it's chosen and at the end. Everything in
  between is cold.
- **Sound:** a music box carries the theme. The stadium roar happens to the others. During the stand, all sound
  drops except the hits and the clock ticks. The horn sounds at 0:00. No laugh track; there's nothing to laugh at.
- **Runtime:** about 35 s at 30 fps, roughly 1,050 frames, rendered the same way as 7 CENTS.
