## Gameplay lessons from the official "Pro Tips" video
Pushes have three roles: **Tank = "the Bagholder"** (Whale, Chillhouse, Giga), **Healer = "the Bailout"** (Pippin, Cupsey, Apu), **Damage = "the Degens"** (LMAO!, Pepe, Alon).
1. **Drop the tank in the back** with a full bar of COPIUM. By the time it reaches the bridge you can afford the squad.
2. **Stack your squad behind it.** Healer and damage go right behind the tank as it reaches the bridge.
3. **Splash eats swarms.** USELESS Army on your tank? One LMAO! wipes it out.
4. **The Bailout keeps it pumping.** The healer keeps the tank alive.
5. **Pull the tank to the middle.** LASER EYES in the middle: their tank turns to chase it, and both your towers get free shots.
6. **Rug the support, not the tank.** Spells go on the squishy units behind; the tank then walks into your Laser Eyes alone.
7. **Counter-push = free gains.** A defender that survives should walk over the bridge with support stacked behind it.
8. **Never ape in alone.** One shooter at the bridge with no tank in front is "exit liquidity".
9. **They went all in? Hit the other lane.** A big tank in their back means they're out of COPIUM.
