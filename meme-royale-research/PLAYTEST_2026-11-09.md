## What changed since the first playtest (Nov 2–5)
- **An account is now required before you can play.** "TAP TO PLAY" opens a CREATE YOUR ACCOUNT form (name 3–14 + password, no email). Before, you played as a guest and the account was optional in Settings.
- New players start with 0 coins, 100 gems, Clout 0, Level 1 and the Basement arena. The starting deck is Trollface, LMAO!, FUD, Jean Phil, Chill Guy, USELESS, KET and RUG PULL.
- Matchmaking takes 2–14 s. Every opponent was a fresh-looking account at 0–42 Clout with a generated-sounding name (Sleepy Gremlin, Sad Andy, pump_holder, lilgoblin). The server hides a `bot` flag, so some or all of these are probably bots padding the queue. The 3:00 match goes into an overtime phase at COPIUM x2.
- A win gives about +28 Clout, about +26 coins, a bag slot and +4 XP. A loss costs about −22 Clout and gives +1 XP.
- The Trophy Road now goes to 50,000 (it was 47,850 in the V2 trailer).

## Notes for playing it headless
The page is too heavy for software WebGL. Load it, then call `window.__game.stage.render = () => {}`, and play with
`window.__game.match.b.play(0, slot, x, z)`. The field is 18 × 32, your side is z 16–32, and the lanes are at x 3.5 and 14.5.
Card stats are in `data.js` (`CARDS`). The strategy follows the official Pro Tips video.
